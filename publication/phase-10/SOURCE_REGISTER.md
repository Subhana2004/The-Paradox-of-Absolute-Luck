# Phase 10 verified public sources (consulted 7 Nov 2026)

- Chalkdust writer guidance: https://chalkdustmagazine.com/write-for-us/ — audience, typical 500–2000 words, preferred format and editorial contact. Search index recently listed Issue 25 deadline 1 January 2027; cached full page sometimes showed older Issue 24 deadline. Verify directly before submission.
- MAA Math Horizons: https://maa.org/mathhorizons/ — first-year-undergraduate reader, English, format-free LaTeX/Word, editor contact and submission entry.
- Taylor & Francis AI policy for authors: https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/ — does not permit AI-generated manuscript first drafts or sections; declarations and human accountability.
- Taylor & Francis general AI policy: https://taylorandfrancis.com/our-policies/ai-policy/ — disclosure, author responsibility and policy caveats.
- Oxford Lifelong Learning 2026 Tom Rocks Maths competition: https://lifelong-learning.ox.ac.uk/about/tom-rocks-maths-essay-competition — 2026 closed 13 April 2026; PDF-only, max 2000 words. Not evidence of 2027 schedule/rules.
- Journal of Humanistic Mathematics policies: https://scholarship.claremont.edu/jhm/policies.html — no fees, double-anonymous screening, existing-publication rules and explicit ban on primarily AI-written work.
- MAA expository outlets: https://maa.org/publications/ — Mathematics Magazine scope.
- arXiv submission guidance: https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html — topical/refereeable, endorsement/moderation requirements.

**This is a publisher/venue source register, not an editor reply or live submission approval.** Current fees, word ceilings, competition policies, and future deadlines can change; unverified values remain marked unknown.
