# The Paradox of Absolute Luck

**Can probability favor everyone?** A rigorous mathematical exploration of competing guarantees, dependence, exchangeability, and the allocation of reliability—motivated by the fictional *Absolute Luck* ability in Sehoon Kim's *Hardcore Leveling Warrior*.

> **Scope:** An original **expository mathematical investigation**, not a claim of new probability theorems. Numerical examples and simulated outcomes are synthetic mathematical models, **not measurements or verified mechanics of the webtoon**.

## The two paradoxes

1. **Luck collision:** Two players each claiming a 99% chance of winning a *single mutually exclusive contest* describe an impossible common probability model, but each may coherently win 99% against a different baseline opponent.
2. **Luck multiplication:** Two hundred individual events may each have a 99.5% success probability even though their *joint* success has probability zero, given a particular finite dependence structure. Different dependence assumptions produce different joint outcomes.

Additional explorations address higher-order dependence, finite exchangeability and extendibility, and probability-boost allocation under different couplings.
